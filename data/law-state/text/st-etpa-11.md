Emergency Tenant Protection Act 576/74 (Open Legislation law id ETP, Chapter 1974) > § 11 Non-waiver of rights

Source: https://legislation.nysenate.gov/api/3/laws/ETP/11 | Open Legislation activeDate: 2019-06-28 | published versions: 2014-09-22, 2015-06-26, 2015-07-03, 2019-06-28

  § 11. Non-waiver of rights. Any provision of a lease or other rental
agreement which purports to waive a tenant's rights under this act or
regulations promulgated pursuant thereto shall be void as contrary to
public policy.
