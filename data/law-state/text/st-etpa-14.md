Emergency Tenant Protection Act 576/74 (Open Legislation law id ETP, Chapter 1974) > § 14 Application of act

Source: https://legislation.nysenate.gov/api/3/laws/ETP/14 | Open Legislation activeDate: 2019-06-28 | published versions: 2014-09-22, 2015-06-26, 2015-07-03, 2019-06-28

  § 14. Application of act. The provisions of this act shall be
applicable:
  a. in the city of New York; and
  b. in all counties within the state of New York outside the city of
New York and shall become and remain effective only in a city, town or
village located therein as provided in section three of this act.
