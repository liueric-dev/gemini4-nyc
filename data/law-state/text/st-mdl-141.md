NY Multiple Dwelling Law (Open Legislation law id MDW, Chapter 61-A) > Article 5 Non-fireproof Multiple Dwellings > Title 1 Fire Protection > § 141 Height

Source: https://legislation.nysenate.gov/api/3/laws/MDW/141 | Open Legislation activeDate: 2014-09-22 | published versions: 2014-09-22

  § 141. Height. Any such dwelling which is seventy-five feet or less in
height above the curb level and has six stories or less may be of
non-fireproof construction.
