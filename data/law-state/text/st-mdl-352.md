  § 352. Recovery of premises.  If a multiple dwelling, or any part
thereof, shall be used as a house of prostitution or assignation with
the permission of the lessee or his agent, the lease shall be terminable
at the election of the lessor, and the owner shall be entitled to
recover possession of said premises by summary proceedings.
