  § 283. Occupancy permitted. Notwithstanding any other provision of
this chapter or any other law, code, rule or regulation, occupancy for
residential purposes of residential units covered by this article is
permitted, if such occupancy is in compliance with this article. Nothing
contained herein shall be construed to limit local authorities from
issuing vacate orders for hazardous conditions, if appropriate.
