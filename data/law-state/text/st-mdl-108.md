NY Multiple Dwelling Law (Open Legislation law id MDW, Chapter 61-A) > Article 4 Fireproof Multiple Dwellings > Title 1 Fire Protection > § 108 Partitions

Source: https://legislation.nysenate.gov/api/3/laws/MDW/108 | Open Legislation activeDate: 2014-09-22 | published versions: 2014-09-22

  § 108. Partitions.  All partitions shall rest directly upon the
fireproof floor construction and never upon any wood flooring, and shall
extend to the fireproof construction of the floor or roof above.
