# Rules of the City of New York > Title 28: Housing Preservation and Development > Chapter 43: [Notification by Mortgagee Commencing an Action to Recover Residential Real Property] > § 43-05 [Definition.]

§ 43-05 [Definition.]
The term "mortgagee" as used in these rules has the meaning set forth in § 27-2109.1(a)(1) of the Administrative Code of the City of New York.
