# NYC Administrative Code > Title 28: New York City Construction Codes > Chapter 11: The New York City Electrical Code > Chapter 11: The New York City Electrical Code > Article 1101: Enactment and Update of the New York City Electrical Code > 86.3 Testing.

86.3 Testing.
Electrical work and installations shall be tested as required in this code and in accordance with sections 86.3.1 through 86.3.3. Tests shall be conducted by the department, as applicable.
