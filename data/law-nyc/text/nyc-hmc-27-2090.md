# NYC Administrative Code > Title 27: Construction and Maintenance > Chapter 2: Housing Maintenance Code > Subchapter 4: Administration > Article 1: Powers and Functions of the Department > § 27-2090 Power to make regulations.

§ 27-2090 Power to make regulations.
The department shall have power to promulgate such regulations as it may consider necessary or convenient to interpret or carry out any of the provisions of this code.
