# Rules of the City of New York > Title 24: Department of Health and Mental Hygiene > New York City Health Code > Article 151: Pest Prevention and Management > § 151.05 Notices of Violation.

§ 151.05 Notices of Violation.
(a) Designation of Environmental Control Board. In accordance with Section 1049-a(c)(2) of the Charter, a notice of violation ("NOV") citing a violation of any provision of this Article may be made returnable to the Environmental Control Board in addition to the Health Tribunal at OATH.
(b) Service of a notice of violation. A notice of violation shall be served by delivery to an owner or other person in control of property or premises, upon a member of the partnership, limited liability company or other group, upon an officer, director or managing agent of a corporation, or upon any other person of suitable age and discretion owning or in control of such property. Service may be made to such person(s) at the address of the premises that is the subject of the NOV or at such person's last known residence or business address,
(1) By personal delivery in accordance with Article 3 of the New York Civil Practice Law and Rules or Article 3 of the Business Corporations Law; or
(2) By mail delivered by the U.S. Postal Service or by any other type of mailing service. Documentation of mailing shall be sufficient proof of service of the notice of violation.
