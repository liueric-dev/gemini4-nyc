86.4 Sign-off of completed work.
If, after inspection, such wiring or appliances shall be found to have been installed, altered, or repaired in conformity with the requirements of this code, and the rules of the department, and the required fees paid, the commissioner shall issue to the applicant a sign-off of the approved work completed.
