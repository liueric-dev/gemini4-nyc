80.2 Scope.
The provisions of this code shall apply to the installation, alteration, maintenance, repair, or demolition of electric wires and wiring apparatus and other appliances used or to be used for the transmission of electricity for electric light, heat, power, signaling, communication, alarm, or data transmission.
