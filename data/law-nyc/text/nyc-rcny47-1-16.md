# Rules of the City of New York > Title 47: Commission on Human Rights > Chapter 1: Practice and Procedure > Subchapter B: Complaints, Answers, and Notification of Obligations > § 1-16 Rebuttal Statements.

§ 1-16 Rebuttal Statements.
Upon request from the Law Enforcement Bureau, a complainant may submit a rebuttal to a respondent's answer and position statement.
(Added City Record 8/6/2019, eff. 9/5/2019)
