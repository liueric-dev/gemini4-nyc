# NYC Administrative Code > Title 28: New York City Construction Codes > Article 312: Carbon Monoxide, Smoke and Natural Gas Alarms > § 28-312.6 Location of smoke alarms and smoke detectors.*

§ 28-312.6 Location of smoke alarms and smoke detectors.*
* Editor's note: there are two sections designated as § 28-312.6.
On or after January 1, 2021, smoke alarms and smoke detectors installed or replaced in group R occupancies shall be installed in accordance with section 907.2.11.6 of the New York city building code.
(L.L. 2018/113, 6/9/2018, eff. 6/9/2018; Am. L.L. 2021/126, 11/7/2021, eff. 11/7/2022; Am. L.L. 2023/077, 6/11/2023, eff. 6/11/2023)
Editor's note: For related unconsolidated provisions, see Appendix A at L.L. 2021/126 and L.L. 2023/077.
