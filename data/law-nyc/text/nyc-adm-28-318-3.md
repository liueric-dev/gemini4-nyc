# NYC Administrative Code > Title 28: New York City Construction Codes > Article 318: Periodic Inspection of Gas Piping Systems > § 28-318.3 Inspection process.

§ 28-318.3 Inspection process.
Gas piping systems shall be inspected and tested in accordance with sections 28-318.3.1 through 28-318.3.4.
(L.L. 2016/152, 12/6/2016, eff. 12/6/2016)
