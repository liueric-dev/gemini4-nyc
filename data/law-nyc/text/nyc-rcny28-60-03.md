# Rules of the City of New York > Title 28: Housing Preservation and Development > Chapter 60: CityFHEPS Rent Stabilized Unit Repair Program > § 60-03 Certified Reasonable Cost Schedule.

§ 60-03 Certified Reasonable Cost Schedule.
HPD will publish a Certified Reasonable Cost Schedule on its website, setting forth the maximum reimbursement limits on Eligible Construction based on analysis of the RSMeans database or similar database of construction costs.
(Added City Record 11/16/2023, eff. 12/16/2023)
