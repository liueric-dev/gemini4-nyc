# NYC Administrative Code > Title 20: Consumer and Worker Protection > Subchapter 15: Rental Real Estate Agreements > § 20-699.25 Outreach and education.

§ 20-699.25 Outreach and education.
a. The commissioner shall establish an outreach and education campaign about the provisions of this subchapter. Such outreach and education shall be provided to real estate brokers, tenants, prospective tenants and members of the public who are likely to be affected by this law.
b. The materials required by this section shall be made available on the department’s website in English and the designated citywide languages as provided in section 23-1101.
(L.L. 2024/119, 12/13/2024, eff. 6/11/2025)
