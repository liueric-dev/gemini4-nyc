# NYC Administrative Code > Title 28: New York City Construction Codes > Article 312: Carbon Monoxide, Smoke and Natural Gas Alarms > § 28-312.1 General.

§ 28-312.1 General.
Required carbon monoxide, smoke and natural gas alarms shall comply with the provisions of this article.
(Am. L.L. 2016/157, 12/6/2016, eff. 5/1/2019)
Editor's note: For related unconsolidated provisions, see Appendix A at L.L. 2013/112 and L.L. 2016/157.
