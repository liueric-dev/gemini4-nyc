# Rules of the City of New York > Title 28: Housing Preservation and Development > Chapter 13: Stays of Legal Action Regarding Violations of the Multiple Dwelling Law and the Housing Maintenance Code > § 13-10 Severability.

§ 13-10 Severability.
In the event that any section or portion of this chapter shall be held invalid or unenforceable for any reason, it shall not in any way invalidate, affect or impair the remaining sections of this chapter.
