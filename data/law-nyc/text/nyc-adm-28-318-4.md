# NYC Administrative Code > Title 28: New York City Construction Codes > Article 318: Periodic Inspection of Gas Piping Systems > § 28-318.4 Fees.

§ 28-318.4 Fees.
The department may charge filing fees for the certifications required by section 28-318.3.3, as set forth in the rules of the department.
(L.L. 2016/152, 12/6/2016, eff. 12/6/2016)
