81.4 Design, installation, alterations, or repairs.
The design, installation, alteration, or repair of electric wires and wiring apparatus and other appliances used or to be used for the transmission of electricity for electric lights, heat, power, signaling, communication, alarm, or data transmission shall conform to the requirements of this code. Alterations or repairs shall not cause an existing installation to become unsafe, hazardous, or overloaded.
